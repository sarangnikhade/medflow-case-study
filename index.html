02
Research Plan

What we set out to learn

Research Questions
RQ1

What does a typical medication routine look like day-to-day?

RQ2

What causes people to skip or delay doses — intentionally or not?

RQ3

What support systems exist and where do they break down?

RQ4

How does medication complexity affect behavior?

Methods + Rationale

Qualitative Interviews

Phase 1 · First

6–8 participants · 45–60 min each

Rationale

Discover real barriers before assuming answer options.

Moderated Usability Testing

Phase 2 · Later

5 participants · early prototype

Rationale

Sensitive health topic — need real-time “why?” probing.

Surveys

Excluded

Discovery phase only

Rationale

Without interview findings, answer options would reflect our assumptions — not users’ reality.

Recruitment Criteria
Adults managing at least one daily prescription
Mix of chronic conditions
Must include people who struggle with adherence
03
Interview Guide

Summary view

8 questions · ~45–60 min
Warm-up
Q1

Tell me about yourself — what does a typical weekday look like?

Opens the conversation · establishes context
Core questions
Q2

How long have you been taking your medications and how did you get started?

Q3

Walk me through your medication routine on a normal day.

Q4

Tell me about a time your routine felt difficult or got disrupted.

Q5

Who or what plays a role in your medication routine?

Q6 Core insight question

Have you ever — consciously or not — skipped or delayed a dose? Tell me about that.

Q7

If you could change one thing about how you manage medications, what would it be?

Q8

Is there anything about managing medication most people wouldn’t understand?
